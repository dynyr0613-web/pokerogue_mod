  (licenses/code/GPL-3.0-only.txt), cleaned into plain pixel art (hard edges, a small palette of each icon's own
  colours). Recoloured from PKHeX's: the Golden Alpha Mark, the six stat Effort Ribbons, the Grand Footprint Ribbon;
  the Endless Master Ribbon uses its Tower Master Ribbon and the Endless milestone ribbons its Gen 4 Ability
  Ribbons. From PokeRogue's own icons: the Egg Move, Master Ball and Eternatus Marks and the three shiny marks
  (the Shiny Charm recoloured, grown 15%). The Perfect IV Mark is PokéSprite's Gold Bottle Cap, doubled.
  Mark and ribbon names and titles are the main series' own (checked in Poké Corpus,
  https://abcboy101.github.io/poke-corpus/).
