kyledove trainer sprites: Pokengine Public Use Policy

Source: Pokengine Public Use Policy, https://pokengine.org/legal/public-use-policy
(last updated 2026-09-23; read on 2026-11-10). That page is the authoritative text; this is a summary of it.

- kyledove's trainer battle sprites are available for public use in the policy's "Free to Use With Credit" category:
  non-commercial use, with credit to the creator, pointing people to Pokengine (https://pokengine.org) as the source.
- Only simple recolours are allowed: no other edits, no cutting the sprites up for parts, no new characters made from
  them, no recolours that change a character's race or skin tone.
- The sprites may not be redistributed as a standalone resource collection, database or asset library.
- They may not be used for, or incorporated into, generative AI work in any way (inputs, training, references,
  AI edits or derivatives).
- kyledove may refuse use to any project or person.
- The Pokemon Showdown trainer sprite index, from which the files were downloaded
  (https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers/), adds: credit the artist, and do not edit the sprites without
  permission.

This fork uses the files unedited, inside the game only, in a private, non-commercial build, and credits the artist
and Pokengine in CREDITS.md. The characters themselves are owned by Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK Inc.
